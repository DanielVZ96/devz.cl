## Frequently asked questions

{{< details summary="Where is my data stored?" >}}
Everything stays on your device. GrownApp doesn't use an account and doesn't upload anything — your tasks and history never leave your iPhone.
{{< /details >}}

{{< details summary="How do I change the language?" >}}
Open the **You** tab and pick **Español** or **English**. The whole app switches immediately.
{{< /details >}}

{{< details summary="I'm not getting reminders." >}}
Two things to check: first, make sure notifications are enabled for GrownApp in **iOS Settings → GrownApp → Notifications**. Second, in the **You** tab check your reminder style — you can get a daily digest, a nudge before each task, or a notification on the due date.
{{< /details >}}

{{< details summary="Can I pause or delete a task?" >}}
Yes. Open a task's detail view to edit it, pause it, or delete it. Paused tasks stop generating reminders but stay in your list until you're ready.
{{< /details >}}

{{< details summary="How do I start over or reset my data?" >}}
You can delete tasks and history from the app. To wipe everything and redo onboarding, delete and reinstall GrownApp — since data is stored locally, that fully resets the app.
{{< /details >}}

{{< details summary="Can I add my own tasks?" >}}
Absolutely. Use the "+" button in the "Today" section to create a custom task with its own repeat interval (days, weeks, months, or years) and reminder lead time.
{{< /details >}}
