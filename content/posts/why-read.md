Adults barely read anymore. *I* barely read anymore. But we adults do watch a lot of Youtube. At least *I* do. I just finished listening to a few videos while vacuuming. One [was about US intervention to support the Yen](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nxRuyHPTzWU), another one about [the rise of pseudo-intellectualism](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_fr6Vxb3vS8), and just before sitting down to write a bit, I was watching [this video on the rise of "cultural nihilism"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qjna7PROMZ8).

> Yikes. Youtube really knows how to design my algorithm.

They weren't bad videos at all. But to me it does feel like it'd have been better to just vacuum while being with my own thoughts. Also I'd feel better with myself if instead of sitting through a 40 minutes long video essay, I'd have used that time to learn maths or read. But here we are. It's 9:30pm and I need to go to bed soon. 

Why do I do this to myself? Why can't I just be considerate with future-me?

There's one side of my brain that really enjoys watching videos. Specially the kind of videos that I feel like help me learn something. But there's another side that wants to truly learn, further than for example just knowing there some elementary particles called gluons, but at some point really get to understand at least the basic theory behind them, maths included.

One side of me wants to read and learn. But another side answers with: *Why read when few video do trick?*.

### To answer myself:

Read because it's better. It's faster. It lets me go at my own pace. It respects my time. It is how I actually learned to program (by reading documentation) and lifted me from tutorial hell. Videos are usually a distillation of written content. The only issue with reading is that it doesn't hack my dopamine like videos do.
