I stumbled upon this post from Parker Adey about eating fruits whole or including the skin: https://pgadey.ca/blog/eating-fruit-skins/

I've been eating the entire apple my whole life. Nobody told me I couldn't so I just did. I only noticed when it was already too late and to me not eating the core felt like a waste.

About 7 years ago a colleague told me kiwis taste better eaten with the skin. I trusted him and to my surprise he is right! I don't like kiwis that much; there's some specific acidity of them that I don't enjoy. But somehow the skin makes it so much more tolerable, and it lets me maximize the amount of the sweet section of the kiwi I eat.

So I've learned that default eating habits are not always for the best.
